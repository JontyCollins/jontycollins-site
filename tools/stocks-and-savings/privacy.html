Privacy notice

Money Health Check (concept demo) · Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version: this is a demo. Everything you type is worked out on your own device and never leaves it. The email box isn't connected to anything yet, so nothing you enter is sent or stored.

Who runs this page

This Money Health Check is a concept demo built by Jonty Collins as a proposal for Stocks and Savings. It isn't run by Stocks and Savings and isn't live yet. If you have any questions about this notice, email jonty@jontycollins.com.

Your answers and your report

The figures you enter (age, income, savings, pension and so on) are used only to do the calculations in your web browser. Your report is built on your device and downloaded straight to it. None of these figures are sent to us or anyone else, and they're gone when you close the page.

Your name and email

In this demo, the name and email boxes aren't connected to any mailing list or database. Nothing you type there is sent or saved.

Services the page uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts this page. Like any website host, it may briefly log technical details such as your IP address for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) provides the code that creates your PDF.

These services receive the standard technical information every web request includes (such as your IP address and browser type) so they can deliver their files. They don't receive any of your answers.

Cookies

This tool doesn't set any cookies or use tracking or analytics.

If this goes live

If Stocks and Savings launches the tool, it would collect your email (and first name if you give it) to send your report and their newsletter, but only with your permission, and you could unsubscribe at any time. Stocks and Savings would then be responsible for that information and would publish its own privacy notice, explaining how it's stored, how long it's kept and how to exercise your rights.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what's held about you and to have it deleted. As this demo doesn't collect or store personal data, there's nothing held about you. You can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

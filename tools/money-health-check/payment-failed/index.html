Your payment didn't go through

No problem, it happens. Your order wasn't completed, so you haven't been charged for it.

If you see a pending amount on your card, it's usually a temporary hold that your bank releases automatically within a few days.

Common fixes

  • Check the card number, expiry date, security code and postcode
  • Make sure your bank hasn't blocked the payment (some banks ask you to approve online purchases in their app)
  • Try a different card, or PayPal
